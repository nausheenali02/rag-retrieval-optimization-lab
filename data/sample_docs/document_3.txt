
Hybrid search combines semantic search with keyword-based search. Semantic search finds conceptually similar content using vector embeddings, while keyword search identifies exact words or phrases.

BM25 is a keyword-based ranking algorithm used in information retrieval. It scores documents based on query-term matches and factors such as term frequency and document length.

Reranking improves retrieval by reordering an initial set of candidate documents according to their relevance to a query. A reranker can help move the most useful chunks toward the top of the results.

Metadata filtering restricts retrieval to documents that satisfy conditions such as category, source, or document identifier. Query rewriting transforms a user's question into a form that may retrieve more relevant information.
